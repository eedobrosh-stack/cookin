How a Pawed story gets made

A reader adds the podcasts they care about. The server watches them, turns each new episode into a written story in Hebrew and English, and serves it back. The Mac steps in only when the server can't do a step.

The story in eight steps

  1. Add sources. A signed-in reader adds channels, podcasts or one episode. Pawed also keeps its own roster.
  2. Watch. The server checks YouTube every 10 minutes and podcast feeds every 20.
  3. Queue. Each new episode becomes a job. A failed job retries on its own.
  4. Get the words. YouTube: captions from Supadata. Podcasts up to 40 minutes: Gemini listens. Everything else goes to the Mac.
  5. Write. LunaRoute writes the story, with quotes and timestamps.
  6. Translate. A background loop adds the English edition.
  7. Publish. The server serves the home page, article pages and the news API.
  8. Read. The flag switch picks the language; it is remembered.

When something looks stuck

  • Queue page (/vidsum/logs) shows what is waiting and who handles it.
  • Deleted or private video: marked unavailable and not retried.
  • Live stream not started: waits until it ends and has captions.
  • Supadata over its plan: the server logs it and pauses 30 minutes; the Mac covers.
  • Deploys are manual: Render, Manual Deploy, Deploy latest commit.
